## Decision logic (current)

| Condition | Decision |
|-----------|----------|
| mean > go_t | Go |
| mean < kill_t | Kill |
| between thresholds AND similarity_score >= 0.65 | Repurpose |
| between thresholds, similarity < 0.65 | Continue |
